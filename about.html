OUR STORY

From a simple idea
to a shared table.

OLIVA began in 2019 with a notebook full of recepies and a simple belief: the best meals make you feel at gome, no matter where their inspiration comes from.
After years spent in kitchens across Europe and beyond, chef Miguel Santos returned to Lisbon with a desire to bring those experiences together. In a quiet corner of the city, he created a place where Portuguese hospitality meets a wonderfully curious kitchen.
Today, that spirit lives in every place - and every person who joins our table.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Curiosity in the kitchen.
Care in every detail.

We see contemporary gastronomy as a conversation between places, people and seasons.
Quality is our starting point. Genuine hospitality is what brings it all together. from your welcome to your last bite, we want your time at OLIVA to feel personal, generous and unhurried.

INGREDIENTS & QUALITY

Good food starts
with good roots.

We choose our ingredients with care: seasonal produce from local growersm responsibly sourced fish and the finest olive olis. Our menu follows what is fresh, not just what is fashionable.
Prepared thoughtfully and presented simply, each dish lets its ingredients speak. A little precision. A little imagination. Nothing unnecessary.
